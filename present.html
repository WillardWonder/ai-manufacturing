Watch it work

Every shop has one

Meet Dale.

31 years on Press 4. The only one who really knows the die change. Retiring in two years. A quarter of Wisconsin's manufacturing workforce is approaching retirement.

Watch it work

Dale's voice memo, word for word

"Once it's locked out the way the tag on Press 4 says, pull the old die. Use the cart, not the forklift. Clean the bolster, really clean it, one chip and your parts are out. Set the new die, line up the pins, snug the clamps, then torque them. There's a number on the sheet in the office. Check shut height, every time. Inch it through, run one part, call quality before you run."

This part isn't AI.The knowledge comes from a person. Recorded on a phone, transcribed for free.

Watch it work

Ask vaguely

Write an SOP for a die change.
Purpose: This procedure ensures safe and efficient die changes in accordance with company standards… Remove the die using appropriate lifting equipment…

It filled in every blank.None of it is Dale's. "Appropriate lifting equipment"? Dale said use the cart, never the forklift.

Watch it work

Give it the context only you have

Turn the transcript into a one-page work instruction for the Press 4 die change. Who it's for: operators with less than a year of experience. Some read English as a second language. Format: numbered steps, one action each. Rules: Start by stating the press must be locked out using its posted lockout procedure. Don't write lockout steps. Use only what's in the transcript. If something is missing, list it as a question.

No magic words.Who it's for. What it should look like. What it must not do.

Watch it work

What came back

BEFORE YOU START: Press 4 must be locked out using its posted lockout procedure. This instruction does not replace it. 1. Remove old die with the cart. No forklift. 2. Clean the bolster completely. 3. Set new die. Line up locating pins. 4. Snug clamps, then torque to the setup sheet. 5. Check shut height before cycling. 6. Inch through one full stroke. 7. Run one part. Call Quality for first article.
Questions for the supervisor

What is the clamp torque value?

What is the shut height for each die?

Who verifies the lockout before the die is pulled?

Know where AI stops

AI can draft work instructions. Your safety process owns safety procedures.

Lockout is regulated: OSHA requires written, machine-specific energy control procedures, verified before work starts and inspected every year. That's why the draft points to the posted procedure instead of writing its own lockout steps, and why a person always signs off.

AI drafts.
People decide.

Before you buy

Advice from NIST's program for small manufacturers

01

One problem

Downtime, scrap, or quality. One focused, measurable problem.

02

Simple fix first

Root cause, a checklist, a standard procedure, training. Compare before buying AI.

03

Business numbers

Measure scrap, throughput, downtime. Not "accuracy."

04

A real pilot

Weeks, not a day. Normal runs and rare ones. Plan for ongoing cost.

"98% accurate" can mean useless.A defect model that calls every part "good" is 98% accurate when 2% are bad.

Before you buy

Questions to ask any AI vendor

  • Where has this worked on a problem like ours?
  • What data does it need, and in what shape?
  • When does it fail, and how will we know?
  • How does it connect to our ERP and maintenance system?
  • What's the full cost: licenses, usage, setup, people, upkeep?
  • Who monitors and retrains it after go-live?
  • Where does our data go, and is it used to train your models?
  • Can we run a paid pilot before we commit?
Guardrails

Most problems come from what goes in, and what nobody checks

Don't paste in

  • Customer drawings or pricing under NDA
  • Export-controlled data
  • Employee personal or medical info
  • Passwords and system access

Always check

  • Every number: torque, tolerances, quantities
  • Every safety step
  • Names, dates, part numbers, sources
  • Translations, with a fluent speaker
Guardrails

If you wouldn't email it to a stranger, don't paste it in.

Free accounts may use your conversations unless you change the settings. Business plans generally protect more. Read the terms.
